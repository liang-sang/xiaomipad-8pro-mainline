待机深度睡眠、开机即有正确时间，GPU 计算继续提速。

## 更新

- **待机深度睡眠**：系统睡眠时，整颗芯片现在会进入深度省电状态：主逻辑电源 CX 断电，内存自刷新。此前版本睡眠时芯片一直没有进入这一级。为此，音频模块闲置时会释放 DSP 上的硬件资源，与原厂一致；修正了上游 SM8750 系统缓存表中 DSP 岛分片的配置；PCIe、UFS、串口和 GPU 电源管理单元在睡眠时也会释放各自的资源。
- **时间保持**：硬件时钟的时间偏移按原厂方式存放在电源管理芯片里，首次联网校时后，重启和在 Android 之间切换都保持正确时间，开机即有正确时间。
- **GPU 计算**：Mesa 编译器在计算着色器里把逐字节读取合并为整字读取后复用，图像处理类计算热点约快 27–29%。
- **桌面默认**：屏幕默认 120 Hz（可在设置里改为 144 Hz）；电池供电时空闲 5 分钟自动睡眠。

## 已知限制

- 拔下 OTG 设备后，直接插入的 OTG 转接头可能不被识别；插拔一次充电器或电脑线，或改用扩展坞即可。
- 蓝牙偶发需要重新连接才能恢复数据或音频。
- 后摄为固定焦距，近景可能偏虚，颜色调校仍有限。

手写笔、指纹和休眠到磁盘仍不支持。保留默认扬声器音量上限。

Deep sleep in standby, the correct time from boot, and faster GPU compute.

## Changes

- **Deep sleep in standby**: during system sleep the whole chip now enters a deep power-saving state: the main CX logic supply is switched off and memory is in self-refresh. Earlier releases never reached this state in sleep. To get there, the audio blocks release their DSP hardware resources when idle, as the stock software does; the DSP island slice in the upstream SM8750 system cache table is corrected; and PCIe, UFS, the serial ports and the GPU power management unit also release their resources in sleep.
- **Timekeeping**: the hardware clock offset is stored in the power management IC as the stock software does, so after the first network time sync the time stays correct across reboots and switches to Android, and is correct from boot.
- **GPU compute**: the Mesa compiler combines byte-wise reads in compute shaders into word reads and reuses them, making image processing compute hotspots about 27–29% faster.
- **Desktop defaults**: the display defaults to 120 Hz (144 Hz can be selected in Settings); on battery the tablet sleeps after five idle minutes.

## Known limitations

- After an OTG device is unplugged, a plain OTG adapter plugged in next may not be detected; plug a charger or computer cable in and out once, or use a dock.
- Bluetooth occasionally needs reconnecting before data or audio works again.
- The rear camera has fixed focus; nearby subjects may be blurry and color tuning is limited.

Stylus, fingerprint and disk hibernation remain unsupported. Keep the default speaker volume cap.
